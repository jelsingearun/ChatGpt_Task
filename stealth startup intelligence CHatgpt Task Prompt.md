Search across India, including Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Pune, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Gurugram, Noida, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kochi, Indore, Chandigarh, and other startup ecosystems. CLASSIFICATION
Classify each company as:
A. Confirmed stealth: credible evidence it deliberately develops its company/product privately.
B. Semi-stealth: public company identity but product, technology, strategy, or operations partly confidential.
C. Emerging startup: early-stage/low visibility, but stealth status not established.
D. Unverified: insufficient evidence of a genuine operating company.
Do not treat a LinkedIn headline alone as proof. Exclude unverified companies from primary recommendations; optionally list them separately as unverified leads with reasons.

COMPANY AND BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE
For each company research name, industry, classification, founding year, headquarters/locations, website, LinkedIn, legal entity where public, founders/co-founders, business model, product/technology, development stage (idea/prototype/MVP/commercial), target customers, competitors, traction/pilots/customers if verifiable, market opportunity, and latest developments. FOUNDER AND LEADERSHIP INTELLIGENCE
Identify founder, co-founder, CEO, CTO, COO, founding engineers, Head of Operations/People, talent acquisition and relevant recruiters. Provide names, current designation, public LinkedIn URLs, relevant professional background, previous companies, public professional contact details only when explicitly published, current affiliation evidence, and the most relevant person to approach. Never guess email addresses or infer recruiting responsibility without evidence.

FUNDING
Investigate bootstrapped status, angel, pre-seed, seed, Series A/B+, accelerators, grants, strategic investments and VC backing. Record amount, round, announcement date, investors, lead investor and source. Prioritize credible pre-seed/seed/Series A companies, then bootstrapped companies with traction and companies with strong hiring evidence. Funding is not proof of a vacancy or runway. Do not infer financial health from funding announcements alone.

HIRING SIGNALS
Find direct signals (official jobs, careers pages, LinkedIn job posts, recruiter/founder hiring announcements) and indirect signals (team expansion, new employees, funding followed by hiring, product launch, new functions, founder requests for founding employees). Classify each as confirmed vacancy, active recruitment announcement, strong hiring signal, weak/inferred signal, or no identified signal. Never present inferred hiring as a confirmed job.

JOB VERIFICATION
For every opening record exact title, company, location, work arrangement, full-time/internship, experience, education, 2026 batch eligibility, skills, salary/stipend if disclosed, deadline, date posted/observed, current status, official application URL, and evidence. Verify the employer and application link. Classify eligibility as FRESHER ELIGIBLE, LIKELY ELIGIBLE, UNCONFIRMED, or NOT ELIGIBLE. Distinguish publication date from announcement date and unknown dates. Exclude expired, fake, pay-to-apply, commission-only, and unpaid full-time roles. Do not assume an undisclosed stipend is paid.

OUTREACH INTELLIGENCE
For companies without a vacancy, assess whether founder outreach is reasonable. Identify the most relevant founder/leader, public LinkedIn, official careers/contact page, and publicly published company/recruitment email if any. Suggest one outreach angle: entry-level software developer, Founder's Office/business operations, paid internship with a potential PPO, or founding team role. Provide one genuine company-specific reason for contact. Do not mass-message, scrape LinkedIn, bypass access controls, use leaked databases, or generate guessed emails. Do not imply a vacancy exists when none is verified.

MANDATORY NEGATIVE INTELLIGENCE / DUE DILIGENCE
Actively investigate reasons NOT to join or approach each startup:
- Funding/financial: no disclosed funding (information gap, not proof of distress), credible financial distress, salary delays/non-payment reports, unclear compensation, funding uncertainty.
- Founder/leadership: unverifiable background, inconsistent claims, credible misconduct allegations, relevant documented controversies or disputes. A previous failed venture alone is not misconduct.
- Business/product: unclear business model, no identifiable product/problem, no product development evidence, unrealistic claims, lack of market validation.
- Hiring/employee: repeated vacancies, high turnover evidence, replacement hiring, unpaid work, misleading PPO promises, unclear duties, unrealistic expectations, salary delays, employee treatment concerns.
- Legitimacy/recruitment: unverifiable entity, misleading website/claims, fake recruiter/job, recruitment/training/security/registration fees, suspicious hiring process.
- Work culture: credible reports of excessive hours, poor management, lack of mentorship, unclear reporting structure.
- Career development: no relevant learning, no mentorship, unclear progression, unrelated duties, risk of unpaid operational work.
For every finding include description, source URL, publication/observation date, evidence class (VERIFIED, ALLEGED, INFERRED, UNKNOWN), severity and candidate impact. Do not turn allegations into facts. Lack of public information must be called an information gap, not evidence of wrongdoing. Include missing information explicitly.

RISK AND OPPORTUNITY SCORES
Keep scores independent.
Opportunity score /100: fresher suitability 25, hiring signals 20, role relevance 20, funding/financial credibility 15, founder accessibility 10, company legitimacy 10. Bands: 80–100 high priority; 65–79 worth approaching; 50–64 investigate; below 50 low priority.
Risk score /100: 0–19 low; 20–39 moderate; 40–59 high; 60–79 very high; 80–100 critical. Score only when evidence supports it; otherwise use INSUFFICIENT INFORMATION. Do not penalize stealth status itself or lack of disclosure as misconduct. A high opportunity score never cancels critical risk. For critical risk, exclude from recommendations. For high/very high risk, recommend investigation before approach.

Before accepting any offer, flag need to verify legal employer, written offer, compensation, employment terms, responsibilities, probation/notice, internship duration and PPO terms.

QUALITY RULES

Prioritize developments from the previous 90 days; use older sources for background. Cite direct URLs for material claims. Label facts, allegations, inferences, estimates, and unknowns. Report fewer results rather than lowering verification standards. Never recommend paying recruitment/training/security fees or unpaid full-time work. Do not send emails, contact recruiters, or initiate external communication. Provide research and copy-ready outreach guidance only.

2. Is there a real vacancy or credible outreach opportunity?
3. Is it suitable for a fresher?
4. What are the reasons to approach?
5. What are the reasons to avoid?
6. What should the candidate do next?

Target 10–20 qualified discoveries and 5–10 strong opportunities only when evidence supports them. These are targets, not quotas. Accuracy, employer safety, and career value take precedence over volume.
